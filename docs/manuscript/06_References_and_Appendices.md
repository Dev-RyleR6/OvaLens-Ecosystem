## 6.2 Appendix A: Hardware Pinout & Wiring Specification
* **Microcontroller**: ESP32 Dev Module (30-pin)
* **GPIO 14**: Infrared Optical Beam Break Sensor (INPUT_PULLUP, 600ms lockout)
* **GPIO 18**: High-Torque Diverter Servo PWM (115200 baud UART)
* **GPIO 19**: High-Intensity Candling LED Driver Relay
