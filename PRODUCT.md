# OvaLens dashboard

OvaLens supports Foundation University hatchery staff managing Itik Pinas duck egg batches, Day 10 candling records, sorter devices, and Penoy recovery. The backend and PostgreSQL database are the central source of truth; hardware operation remains in the edge application.

The user selected daily operations as the dashboard's priority: clear tables, readable metrics, and restrained styling. Preserve the supplied OvaLens logo, FU maroon, agricultural green, and slate surfaces. Preserve the strict two-class biological model and distinguish low-confidence records requiring inspection.

Operators need to locate batches and scans, identify unavailable or stale data, inspect an egg, and correct its classification with a reason. Administrators also manage users and facility settings. Do not imply that a backend configuration save confirms a physical actuator action. Unimplemented remote hardware controls must be identified as unavailable.
